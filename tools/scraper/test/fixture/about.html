About Brightside Dental

Brightside Dental opened in 2001 with two chairs and a waiting list. Today the practice looks after more than four thousand patients across Leeds, with six dentists and a team of hygienists who have been with us for over a decade.

Our approach

We believe appointments should never feel rushed. Every new patient gets a forty-minute assessment, digital x-rays and a written plan they can take away and think about. If a treatment is not needed, we will say so.

Prevention comes first. Our hygienists spend most of their clinical time helping people avoid treatment rather than delivering it, and we publish our prices so there are no surprises at the desk.

The team

Our dentists train every year in the latest minimally invasive techniques. Two of them specialise in treating nervous patients, including sedation options for longer procedures.

Getting here

We are a two minute walk from Leeds station, with step-free access from Park Row and a ground floor treatment room.
