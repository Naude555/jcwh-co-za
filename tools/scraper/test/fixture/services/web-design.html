Check-ups and hygiene

From £45

A routine examination is the cheapest treatment you will ever have. We check your teeth, gums, soft tissue and bite, take x-rays where they are clinically useful, and record anything that needs watching.

What is included

  • Full examination of teeth, gums and soft tissue
  • Digital x-rays where clinically necessary
  • Scale and polish with a hygienist
  • Written treatment plan and cost estimate

How often should I come?

Most patients are seen every six months. If your gums are stable and your hygiene is good, some patients move to yearly visits. We will tell you which group you are in and why.
