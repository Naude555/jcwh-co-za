Do I really need to come twice a year?

By Dr Priya Raman · 2 June 2026

The six-month recall is one of the most familiar habits in dentistry, but it is a habit rather than a clinical rule. The interval should follow your risk of disease, and that changes over a lifetime.

What we are actually looking for

A check-up is a risk assessment. We look at decay, gum health, wear, existing restorations and the soft tissue. Someone with stable gums, no decay in a decade and good hygiene genuinely does not need to be seen as often as someone with active gum disease.

Who should come more often

Smokers, patients with diabetes, anyone with a history of gum disease and patients wearing orthodontic appliances should all be seen more frequently. So should anyone who has had several fillings in the last two years.

How to move to a longer interval

Ask. We will show you your chart and explain the reasoning. If your risk is low we are happy to move you to yearly appointments, and to bring you back sooner if anything changes.
