Every modernization starts with the same uncomfortable question: how much of this
site actually needs to exist?

## Crawl it

A crawl gives you three things you cannot get any other way: the true page count,
the real internal link graph, and a copy of content that may only live in the CMS
of a vendor you are about to stop paying.

## Count the templates, not the pages

Eight hundred pages is rarely eight hundred designs. Group them by layout and the
work becomes countable: six templates, four section types, one contact form.

## Decide the fate of every asset

Images and documents are where migrations quietly bloat. For each asset decide:
resize and keep, replace with a modern format, or discard. Originals with
sensible dimensions are worth keeping; a 4000px JPEG used at 300px is not.

## Write the redirect map

The redirect map is the deliverable that protects your rankings, and it is
cheapest to produce while the old site is still up. Produce it before launch, not
after.

## Then design

With the inventory in hand, the design brief writes itself: what has to fit, how
many variants exist, and which patterns are worth investing in.
