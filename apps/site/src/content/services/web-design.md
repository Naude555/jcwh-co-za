## What the process looks like

We start with a content audit rather than a mood board. Knowing what actually
has to appear on the site — and what can be retired — is what keeps a rebuild
inside its budget.

1. **Crawl and inventory.** Every page, asset and outbound link from the current
   site is catalogued and grouped.
2. **Design system.** Your colours, typography and spacing become tokens, and the
   section library is designed from real content rather than placeholder copy.
3. **Build.** Pages are composed from the section library, images are optimised,
   and redirects are generated for anything that moved.
4. **Handover.** Your team gets a content guide, a component reference and a
   build that fails loudly if content does not match the schema.

## What you end up with

The result is a site that a designer can extend, a developer can maintain and an
editor can update without breaking anything.
